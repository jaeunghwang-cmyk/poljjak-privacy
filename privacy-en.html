Poljjak Jump Rope Privacy Policy

Jay Company (제이컴퍼니) operates Poljjak Jump Rope for Android, package com.jay.jumprope. This policy covers the Android app. The Toss mini-app has separate platform notices.

Effective September 23, 2026. Operator and privacy contact: Jay Company / ekkk1001@nate.com.

1. Information stored on your device

We store your best score, number of attempts, up to 20 recent attempts (score, time and character), hearts and refill time, badges and stamps, character selections and unlocks, and sound and vibration settings. Optional features ask for an age group (under 13, 13–15, or 16 and up), stored on your device. We do not ask for your date of birth, name, phone number or email to play.

This information remains until you clear app data or uninstall. We do not send your save file to an operator-run server or automatically back it up to the cloud. Test-build reset controls erase the corresponding progress, badges and unlocks.

2. Google Play Games rankings

Versions with rankings enabled use Google Play Games Services. Players under 13 use local records without Google rankings. Other players may choose to sign in using the ranking button. Google handles sign-in and game profiles. Normal best scores are submitted to the leaderboard. Profile and score visibility follows your Google Play Games settings. Test and practice scores are not submitted. Without sign-in or a configured leaderboard, records stay on your device.

Google's SDK may process account and game profile information, game activity, device information and diagnostics to provide these features. See the Google Privacy Policy for handling and retention. Clearing local records does not delete scores already stored by Google. You can manage deletion of game data in Google Play Games settings.

3. Sharing a score

Share score creates text and an image containing your score and opens the Android share sheet. You choose the app and recipient. We do not read contacts or choose recipients automatically. The image is stored in app cache and may be overwritten by the next share or deleted when you clear the cache. Recipient services and people retain shared content under their own policies.

4. Ads, analytics and device features

Ad-enabled versions use Google AdMob rewarded ads. Watching is optional. Completing the ad's reward conditions refills all three hearts. You also receive one free heart every 30 minutes, up to three, without ads.

The ad SDK may send IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), ad interactions, app and device diagnostics, and identifiers to Google for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. We remove Android advertising ID permissions and apply child-directed treatment and a G content rating limit to all ad requests. We do not use a separate analytics SDK. Age groups determine under-age-of-consent treatment. Google consent forms are shown where required; available choices can be managed in Settings → Ad privacy settings. Google's privacy policy governs its retention and deletion practices.

Vibration uses your device's vibration feature. We check silent/vibrate mode to mute sound, without transmitting that setting to a server. We do not request location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions.

Emoji fonts may load from jsDelivr's Tossface CDN, which can receive your IP address and browser request information. See jsDelivr's privacy policy.

5. Contact and deletion requests

If you email us, we use your return address and message to answer and resolve your request. Please do not send sensitive information. We normally delete correspondence after resolution, unless retention is legally required. Send access, correction or deletion requests to the contact above. Delete local data in Android's app storage settings.

6. External services and security

The public policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses and access information under its privacy statement. The app has no operator-run data sale feature. External service communications use their secure connections.

7. Policy updates

We update this page and effective date when practices change, and provide in-app notices or consent where needed. Players and guardians can contact Jay Company at the email above.
